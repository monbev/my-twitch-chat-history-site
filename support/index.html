Support

Most Twitch loading issues can be resolved without reinstalling the extension.

Quick troubleshooting

  1. Confirm that you are signed in to Twitch.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and reload My Twitch Chat History.
  3. Return to Twitch and perform a hard refresh with Ctrl+Shift+R.
  4. Open a channel chat and use Retry if Twitch was still loading.

Reporting a problem

Describe what you expected and what happened. This form sends your report privately to the developer through Telegram. You do not need a Telegram account.

Do not include passwords, tokens, cookies, or private chat messages. The extension does not attach your chat history automatically.
